PRESCOUTING FAN FACTORY — 3D STUDIO CHARACTER PROTOTYPE
Assembled 2026-10-24 for interactive browser preview.

Generator / rigging: Ara 3D Gen by olatunde85/3d-gen, MIT-licensed software.
Base body topology, human morphs and system clothing assets: MakeHuman Community, CC0 1.0.
Skull: Detailed Skull with Articulated Jaw, Innerscene 3D library, CC0 1.0.
Animation: Ara 3D Gen Idle, Wave, Cheer and Laugh keyframed animations; the Walk action is retargeted from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, whose motion files may be used commercially but may not be resold on their own.
The finished characters use distinct body meshes and independently weighted skull meshes with 66-bone humanoid rigs.
The characters are functional art prototypes, not certified sponsor-ready clothing product visualizations.
